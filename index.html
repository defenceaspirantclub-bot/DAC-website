03 · The forces

About NCC and the Defence Forces

The National Cadet Corps (NCC) is the youth wing of the Indian Armed Forces, with Army, Naval and Air Wings. Its motto is “Unity and Discipline.” Cadets learn drill, leadership, weapon training and social service, and earn A, B and C certificates. The C certificate can give benefits in some entries to the forces.

Indian Army

Land force. Entry through NDA, CDS, TES, NCC Special Entry and more.

Indian Navy

Maritime force. Entry through NDA, CDS, INET and other schemes.

Indian Air Force

Air force. Entry through NDA, CDS and AFCAT.

Entry routes

Always check the latest eligibility, age limits and vacancies on the official UPSC and recruitment websites.

04 · Preparation

SSB Interview Guidance

The Services Selection Board (SSB) is a five-day assessment of personality, not just knowledge. We prepare cadets for each stage through regular practice.

  1. ScreeningWe run mock OIR (verbal and non-verbal reasoning) tests and picture perception and discussion (PP&DT) practice.
  2. Psychology testsWeekly practice of TAT, WAT, SRT and Self Description to build quick, natural and positive thinking.
  3. Group Testing Officer tasksGroup discussions, group planning exercise, progressive group tasks and command tasks in the field.
  4. Personal interviewMock interviews with serving and retired officers, with feedback on confidence and honesty.
  5. ConferenceSessions on communication, current affairs, physical fitness and good daily habits.
